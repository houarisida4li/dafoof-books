Questions readers ask most

How do I receive my book?

Every title is a digital download. The files appear the moment payment clears on Payhip, and a link also arrives by email.

Where is my download link?

Log in to Payhip with the same email you bought with: Purchases keeps a download button next to every book you own.

Which payment methods work?

Checkout is handled by Payhip, so you can pay by card or PayPal. No card details ever pass through this site.

Is the free book really free?

Yes — add it to your basket on Payhip and download it. No card needed, no catch.

Can I read the books in Arabic?

The books themselves are in English; this website and the store descriptions are bilingual so you can browse comfortably in either language.
